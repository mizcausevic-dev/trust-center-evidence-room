# Origin

Trust Center Evidence Room exists after the scorecards, board brief, diligence pack, and procurement-answer layers.

The goal is to turn that executive-intelligence estate into one explicit trust-room surface:

- which evidence packs are ready to reuse now
- where artifact coverage or proof freshness is still too weak
- what control evidence is strong enough to support a buyer-safe diligence story
- which routes still need cleaner ownership and packaging before they can convert in procurement, legal, or security review
